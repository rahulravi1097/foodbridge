ABOUT FOODBRIDGE

Good food, shared responsibly.

A coordination platform for surplus food donations.

Our mission

FoodBridge connects food donors with approved NGOs and volunteers so surplus edible food can be discovered, claimed, collected, and tracked instead of wasted.

The project supports SDG 2 (Zero Hunger) and SDG 12 (Responsible Consumption and Production).

SDG 2

Zero Hunger

Make surplus food easier for community organizations to discover and coordinate for people who need support.

SDG 12

Responsible Consumption

Support better handling of surplus food through a visible donation and delivery workflow.

FoodBridge is a coordination tool, not a food-safety certification service. Donors and recipient organizations must follow applicable food-safety requirements.
